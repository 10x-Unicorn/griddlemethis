Melt 2 tbsp of butter on griddle, spreading evenly, add potatoes, Season everything with the garlic and Old bay. Turn so all sides get griddled 3 minutes per side add more butter or oil at 6 min mark. Return to sheet tray.

Serve, Enjoy!
