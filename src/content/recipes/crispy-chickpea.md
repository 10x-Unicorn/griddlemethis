Coat griddle with 2 tbsp of olive oil, spreading evenly, add seasoned chickpeas. Roll/flip chickpeas after 2 min. Flip again and watch carefully for about 2 min. Continue cooking to desired crispiness (6-8 Min for me). Return to sheet tray. Add lemon juice salt and pepper. .

Serve, enjoy!
