Make sure surface is coated lightly with butter. Toast all 8 slices of bread on both sides flipping after 2 min. Griddle the 4 eggs to desired consistency. Place griddled bread on baking sheet, spread each bread slice with a layer of béchamel, spreading it all the way to the edges. Top with parmesan cheese. Bake at 425 degrees F for about 5-6 minutes, until cheese is melted. Remove from oven, and place ham and cheese of 4 of the slices. Top with other 4 slices. (Party hint, you can toast 8 more pieces of bread for bottoms and use the bechamel coated slices for tops to make 8 sandwiches instead of 4, also make 8 piles of ham and cheese vice 4). Then turn the oven to broil and broil until the cheese on top is lightly golden, 2-4 minutes. Top each sandwich with griddled egg and raspberry mustard sauce if desired.

Enjoy!
