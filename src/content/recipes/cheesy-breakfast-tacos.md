Oil griddle, cook sausage patties, smash for a good sear. 4 minutes per side. Flip. Break into small piece, Add the bacon pieces, let cook for a min. then pour in eggs slowly. After eggs are set in about 90 seconds. Move the eggs to the cool part of the griddle. Place 1/4 to 1/3 cup of cheese blend directly on griddle, add same amount of egg mixture on top. Fold over cheese to make a roll. Add to heated tortilla if desired.

Serve, enjoy!
