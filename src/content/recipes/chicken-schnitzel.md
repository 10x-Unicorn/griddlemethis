Melt butter or oil use plenty because you are shallow frying the chicken. Place dredged chicken on the oil. Flip after 4 minutes per side three times (12 min total) till crust is brown and crisp adding more oil if necessary. Remove and place on wire rack till ready to serve and enjoy! Do not forget the sauce!

Serve, enjoy!
