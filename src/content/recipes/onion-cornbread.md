ingredients:
  - heading: "Onion Cornbread"
    items:
      - "1 Box Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix"
      - "1 Cup, onions diced"
      - "1/3 Cup Milk"
      - "1 egg, beaten"
      - "¼ Cup, jalapeños diced (optional)"
      - "4 TBS butter/oil/bacon grease, or as needed for griddling"
  - heading: "Everything Sauce"
    items:
      - "1 TBS Cream Cheese"
      - "1 TBS Sour Cream"
      - "1 TBS Salsa Verde"
steps:
  - heading: null
    items:
      - "Chop onion into ¼ inch dice."
      - "Chop jalapeños into ¼ inch dice."
      - "Beat egg in bowl add milk then corn muffin mix, add onions and jalapeños."
      - "Stir to combine into a thick batter."

Melt butter. Drop 1 TBS of batter onto melted butter. Flip after 4 min (8 min total). Place on cooling rack.

Serve, enjoy!
