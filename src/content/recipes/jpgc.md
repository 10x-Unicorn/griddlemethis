Place your fresh peppers on the griddle with some oil. Let sauté until soft 5 mins. Divide into 4 equal parts. Toast one side of bread 2 min, then flip add sliced cheese to one piece and spread cheese on the other, top that piece with jalapenos. Flip sliced cheese piece on top of that.

Serve, enjoy!
