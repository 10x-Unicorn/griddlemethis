Oil the griddle, griddle the cabbage first for about 5 minutes, flip then mix in the noodles and butter, season with garlic pepper after 5 min flip everything, finish for 5 mins or till you reach your desired crispiness of noodles. Return to sheet tray.

Serve, Enjoy!
