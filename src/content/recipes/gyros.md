title: "Griddle Gyros w/ Tzatziki Sauce"
description: "First things first, it is pronounced (ero, like Hero), not gEYErO, which is an abbreviation for gyroscope, used in navigation. Anyway, Gyros are delicious Greek sandwich. Instead of using a traditional vertical rotisserie, we use the griddle to produce a crispy sear on the meat for this awesome sandwich. The sandwich is constructed with a grilled pita, gyro meat, griddle onions, chopped tomatoes, lettuce, and topped with herb laden tzatziki with a dusting of feta cheese. Spectacular."

ingredients:
  - heading: "Tzatziki"
    items:
      - "1 cup chopped cucumber"
      - "5 oz container plain non-fat Greek yogurt"
      - "1 cup sour cream"
      - "1 tbsp od Red Wine"
      - "1 tsp of Lemon Juice"
      - "1 tbsp chopped fresh dill"
      - "1 tsp of Lemon Juice"
      - "1 tsp minced garlic"
      - "Kosher salt and fresh ground black pepper to taste"
  - heading: "Meat"
    items:
      - "1.5 lbs of meatball mix (or ground meat of your choice, lamb is often used)"
      - "¼ Cup breadcrumbs"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1 tbsp of Granulated Garlic"
      - "1 tsp of Onion Powder"
      - "½ tsp of Salt and Pepper"
      - "½ tsp of Basil"
      - "½ tsp of Oregano"
      - "1 tsp of Lemon Juice"
      - "2 tbsp Olive oil for griddling"
steps:
  - heading: "Tzatziki"
    items:
      - "Chop cucumber and dill."
      - "Stir all ingredients in medium bowl."
      - "Set aside and/or refrigerated."
  - heading: "Meat"
    items:
      - "Scramble eggs into a small bowl."
      - "In large bowl, combine the rest of ingredients and mix with your hands with the eggs."
      - "Make small 3 ounce balls."
      - "Press balls in thin patties about ¼ inch thick."

  performance: []
---

Coat griddle with 2 tbsp of olive oil, spreading evenly, add patties. Flip patties after 5 min or until edges are crispy. Flip again and watch carefully for about 5 min. Return to sheet tray.

Assemble as described above, enjoy! OPA!!!
