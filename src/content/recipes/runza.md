Oil the griddle, griddle first the beef for about 8 minutes, flipping to brown. Place onions and butter separately to brown. Then mix in everything together, adding the liquid ingredients. Let simmer till sauce is thick and reduced about 3 mins. Return to sheet tray. Serve on soft, warm buns.

Enjoy!
