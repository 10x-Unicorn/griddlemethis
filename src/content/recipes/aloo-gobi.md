Have griddle medium hot, like 400F, make sure surface is coated with oil. Place veggies on griddle ensuring all are flat on griddle to maximize sear. Sear for 5 minutes per side, add oil in between Flip every 5 min till all sides are seared, about 20 min. During final 5 mins add butter, let melt add all spices and mix thoroughly.

Enjoy!
