Griddle breakfast meats on hot side of griddle flipping after 2 min (8 min total), shift over to cool side. Melt butter, pour eggs, flip and fold into a square. Griddle bagel. Flip after 2 mins. Add 2 slices of cheese to each piece of bread, spread sauce, and assemble sandwich.

Serve, enjoy!
