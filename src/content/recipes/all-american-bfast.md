Griddle bacon about 3 minutes a side flipping twice, place on wire rack or in half sheet tray with paper towel. Reheat potatoes in bacon grease and chop into smaller pieces in necessary. Cook patties also in grease, press for a good sear. Again 3 minutes per side. Also cook sausage links turning frequently. I set aside 6 links and 6 patties on the rack and chop up the rest for the scramble. Add the bacon pieces, let cook for a min then pour in eggs slowly. After eggs are set in about 90 second, flip and add cheese. Stir in cheese after about 90 seconds and add to half sheet pan.

Serve, enjoy!
