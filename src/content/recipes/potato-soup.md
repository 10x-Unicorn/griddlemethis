Cook bacon over low medium heat, remove when done leaving drippings, Fry potatoes in bacon grease until golden brown. Place in half sheet tray. Sauté onions, carrot, and celery until soft. Place in half sheet tray. Transfer to 5 qt Dutch oven. Add Stock, bacon, vegetables, and milk. Cook until heated through (10 min). Garnish as desired.

Serve, enjoy!
