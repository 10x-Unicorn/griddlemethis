Griddle first the drummies for about 5 minutes, flip then add the flats, after 5 min flip everything, repeat 2 more times until wings reach internal tempo of 160 and are crispy and golden. Alternatively, you can finish in a cast iron pan half full of cooking oil for 5 mins for the restaurant crispiness. Return to sheet tray. Toss in the sauce!

Serve, Enjoy!
