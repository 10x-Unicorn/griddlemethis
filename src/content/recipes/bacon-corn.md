Griddle corn and bacon, add fajita spice, flipping after 3 min (6min total). Move to sheet tray. This recipe can be topped with a ton of extra things. Top with the lime crema. Add anything else you desired, like guacamole, maybe some Cheetos of Fritos for crunch, I do!

Serve, Enjoy!
