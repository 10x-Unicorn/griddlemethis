Melt 1 tbsp of butter on griddle, spreading evenly, add asparagus and cut lemons flat side down. Using steamer lid cover asparagus and add about a TBS of water. Let steam for about 3 min. Flip asparagus and leave uncovered 3 min. Flip again, add salt and pepper, last TBS of butter and squeeze lemons over top. Return to sheet tray.

Serve, enjoy!
